    PokeMMO breeding rules this planner follows

    • Both parents are consumed. The Daycare keeps both parents and any items they hold, so every parent in the tree is used up.
    • Compatibility. Parents must be opposite genders and share an egg group. Ditto breeds with anything that can breed (not with another Ditto). Pokémon in the Undiscovered group can't breed. Genderless Pokémon breed only with Ditto.
    • Species. The egg is the mother's species at its first evolution stage. With Ditto, it is the other parent's species. Nidoran and Volbeat/Illumise are the exception: their eggs hatch as Nidoran♀ or Nidoran♂, Illumise or Volbeat, depending on the egg's gender, so a male egg from a Nidoran♀ is a Nidoran♂.
    • Incense babies. These babies only hatch when a parent holds the matching incense: Sea Incense, Azurill; Lax Incense, Wynaut; Full Incense, Munchlax; Odd Incense, Mime Jr.; Rock Incense, Bonsly; Rose Incense, Budew; Pure Incense, Chingling; Luck Incense, Happiny; Wave Incense, Mantyke. Without it the egg is the next stage (Marill, Wobbuffet, Snorlax and so on). The incense uses that parent's item slot. When your goal is one of these babies, the plan puts the incense in the last breed. Each incense costs $9,600 at the mart.
    • One item per parent. A Power item (brace) guarantees that parent's IV in its stat: Power Weight HP, Power Bracer Attack, Power Belt Defense, Power Lens Sp. Atk, Power Band Sp. Def, Power Anklet Speed. Two parents can lock two different stats.
    • Shared stats carry over. Each IV the egg doesn't take from a parent becomes the parents' average, rounded down. So a stat where both parents are 31 is always 31. That is why every breed in the plan pairs parents that share all but one stat each, and why each extra 31 doubles the number of parents.
    • Nature. A parent holding an Everstone passes its nature. It can't hold a brace in the same breed, which is why a fixed nature roughly doubles the parents needed.
    • Ability. The ability comes from the mother, or from the non-Ditto parent when breeding with Ditto. Hidden abilities pass about 60% of the time. The planner keeps the parent with your ability on the mother's side and shows the overall chance. The regular-ability chance (80%) is the main-series figure; PokeMMO's exact rate is unconfirmed.
    • Egg moves. Egg moves come from the father. The planner puts a father who knows your egg moves in the final breed, and can chain them through other species that also have the move as an egg move.
    • Gender selection. You can pay the Daycare to choose the egg's gender: $5,000 for 1:1 species, $10,000 for 3:1, $25,000 for 7:1. The plan picks a gender whenever the child is used as a parent later, and for the final egg when the Pokémon you want only comes in one gender (Vespiquen, Gallade, Froslass, Nidoking and so on).
    • What the planner counts as guaranteed. Only braced stats and stats both parents share. Any extra 31s from the random third inherited IV are luck and not counted.

    Sources: PokeMMO Wiki: Breeding and PikaMMO breeding guide. Species, egg groups, gender ratios, abilities, egg moves and who can learn each TM come from Pokémon Showdown's data, which can differ from PokeMMO in places; if you spot a mistake, report it. TM prices are mart prices from the PokeMMO Wiki; held item prices are guesses. Box icons, item icons and the Smeargle box icon behind the Smearguru logo are from or based on PokeAPI/sprites.
