      Prices and options

      Prices are estimates, edit them

      Each of your Pokémon counts at half its rough GTL worth (about what selling it brings; the numbers are under the box's worth estimate), and the plan with the lowest cost plus worth used up wins. Plans can take more breeds, but they spend your cheaper breeders first and save the valuable ones.

      Buy parents on the GTL

      Tick what the plan may buy when your Pokémon fall short, and set what each costs. Ones with your nature count when your goal has a nature. A Ditto follows the "Any nature" ticks.

      TMs and held items

      Add what the finished Pokémon needs and it goes into the total. A TM for one of your egg moves means that move no longer has to be bred in.

      Egg IV odds

      Like the game's breeding window

      Type two parents' IVs to see what the egg can get and how likely it is, the way PokeMMO decides it: three IVs are copied from the parents (a braced stat is always one of them) and the other stats get the parents' average, rounded down. So a stat without a brace lands on the lower parent's IV, the average or the higher one, never outside them. Press "Egg odds" under a breed in your plan to fill this in from it.

      PokeMMO breeding rules this planner follows

      • Both parents are consumed. The Daycare keeps both parents and any items they hold, so every parent in the tree is used up.
      • Compatibility. Parents must be opposite genders and share an egg group. Ditto breeds with anything that can breed (not with another Ditto). Pokémon in the Undiscovered group can't breed. Babies like Pichu, Riolu and Happiny can once they evolve, so the plan uses them and says to evolve them first. Genderless Pokémon breed with Ditto or with another Pokémon of their own line, such as Beldum with Metang.
      • Species. The egg is the mother's species at its first evolution stage. With Ditto, it is the other parent's species. Nidoran and Volbeat/Illumise are the exception: their eggs hatch as Nidoran♀ or Nidoran♂, Illumise or Volbeat, depending on the egg's gender, so a male egg from a Nidoran♀ is a Nidoran♂.
      • Incense babies. These babies only hatch when a parent holds the matching incense: Sea Incense, Azurill; Lax Incense, Wynaut; Full Incense, Munchlax; Odd Incense, Mime Jr.; Rock Incense, Bonsly; Rose Incense, Budew; Pure Incense, Chingling; Luck Incense, Happiny; Wave Incense, Mantyke. Without it the egg is the next stage (Marill, Wobbuffet, Snorlax and so on). The incense uses that parent's item slot. When your goal is one of these babies, the plan puts the incense in the last breed. Each incense costs $9,600 at the mart.
      • One item per parent. A Power item (brace) guarantees that parent's IV in its stat: Power Weight HP, Power Bracer Attack, Power Belt Defense, Power Lens Sp. Atk, Power Band Sp. Def, Power Anklet Speed. Two parents can lock two different stats.
      • Shared stats carry over. The egg copies three IVs from its parents (braced stats count among them) and each other IV becomes the parents' average, rounded down, so a stat never lands below the lower parent. So a stat where both parents are 31 is always 31, and one where both have 25 or more is always 25+ (see Egg IV odds). That is why every breed in the plan pairs parents that share all but one stat each, and why each extra 31 doubles the number of parents.
      • Nature. A parent holding an Everstone passes its nature. It can't hold a brace in the same breed, which is why a fixed nature roughly doubles the parents needed.
      • Ability. The ability comes from the mother, or from the non-Ditto parent when breeding with Ditto. Hidden abilities pass about 60% of the time. A male or genderless Pokémon only passes its ability when bred with Ditto. Other abilities pass 80% of the time. The planner keeps the parent with your ability on the mother's side and shows the overall chance.
      • Egg moves. The egg gets every egg move either parent knows, as long as its species can learn it. The planner puts a parent who knows your egg moves in the final breed, and can chain them through other species that also have the move as an egg move (those come in as the father, since the mother sets the species).
      • Gender selection. You can pay the Daycare to choose the egg's gender. The common gender costs $5,000, the same as either gender of a 1:1 species; the rare gender costs $10,000 for 3:1 species and $25,000 for 7:1 (a female Charmander, say). The plan picks a gender whenever the child is used as a parent later, and for the final egg when the Pokémon you want only comes in one gender (Vespiquen, Gallade, Froslass, Nidoking and so on).
      • Alphas. An egg is only alpha when both parents are alpha (with Ditto, an alpha Ditto). Tick Alpha on your goal to breed one: the plan then uses only your alphas. With a normal goal an alpha can still be a parent, and its card shows the alpha mark.
      • What the planner counts as guaranteed. Only braced stats and stats both parents share. Any extra 31s from the random third inherited IV are luck and not counted.

      IV stats

      How this is counted

      Each Pokémon is worth what a similar one goes for on the GTL. 1×31 to 3×31 use the buy prices in the planner's Prices and options (the "with your nature" price when it has a nature that suits its species, the Ditto price for a Ditto). A good nature multiplies 4-6×31, a hidden ability adds a percentage, and egg moves, a 0 Atk or 0 Spe (wanted for special attackers and Trick Room), shiny, alpha and costume add a flat amount. Everything except the planner's prices is a guess you can change here. Blank IVs don't count as 31s.
